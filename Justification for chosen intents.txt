Justification for the Support Manager.
A customer-support manager routes enquiries across four core operational queues:
1. Card Logistics & Activation: 
	card_arrival ("Card delivery"), 
	card_linking ("Card linking"),
	lost_or_stolen_card ("Lost or stolen card"). 

2. Card Payment & Hardware Failures: 
	card_not_working ("Card not working"), 
	declined_card_payment ("Declined card payment"),
	pin_blocked ("PIN blocked").  
	
3. Bank Transfers & Beneficiary Issues: 
	pending_transfer ("Pending transfer"), 
	failed_transfer ("Failed transfer"), 
	transfer_not_received_by_recipient ("Transfer not received").   
	
4. Account Access & ATM Disputes: 
	passcode_forgotten ("Passcode forgotten"), 
	unable_to_verify_identity ("Identity verification failed"), 
	wrong_amount_of_cash_received ("Wrong ATM cash amount").   
	
This selection covers high-volume daily support issues, 
pairs naturally confused intents:
(e.g., card_not_working vs. declined_card_payment, 
and pending_transfer vs. transfer_not_received_by_recipient) 
for meaningful error analysis, and separates cleanly into themes when clustering.
